Made With Filipino Tradition

Palitaw is a traditional Filipino rice cake made from glutinous rice dough. It is commonly served with grated coconut, sugar, and sesame seeds. Palitaw Express brings this familiar Filipino delicacy into a convenient modern food-delivery experience.
